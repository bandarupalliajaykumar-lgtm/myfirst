🌱 About Your Analytics

How these statistics work:

Revenue is calculated from the product totals of orders marked Delivered. It does not include delivery fees, production costs, refunds or profit.

Product rankings use actual delivered quantities. Cancelled and pending orders are excluded from the product sales ranking.

This report covers all orders currently stored for your farmer account. It is not a monthly report and does not use sample forecasting data.
